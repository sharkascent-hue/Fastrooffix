Flat Roof Repairs

What the job involves.

Flat roofs need specialist attention: pooling water, blistered membranes and leaks that show up nowhere near the breach.

We trace the moisture path to the real fault, then repair with torch-on felt or liquid membranes, or replace the covering where that is the better value.

  • Pooling water
  • Blistering and splits
  • Torch-on felt
  • EPDM and fibreglass
