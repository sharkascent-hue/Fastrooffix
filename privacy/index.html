Privacy policy.

How Fast Roof Repairs Dublin handles the details you send us.

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

Fast Roof Repairs Dublin provides roofing and chimney services in County Dublin. If you have any question about this policy or your details, contact us at fastroofrepairsdublin@gmail.com or on 085 173 8320.

What we collect

When you ask for a quote we collect what you give us: your name, phone number, area or Eircode, and optionally your email, the service you need and a description of the problem. If you message or ring us, we also have your number and what you send us.

How we use it

Only to reply to you, arrange an inspection, give you a quote and carry out any work you ask for. We do not sell your details or use them for unrelated marketing. Our lawful basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in answering enquiries.

Who sees it

Our own team. If you send your request through WhatsApp or email, it is carried by that service under its own terms. We do not share your details with anyone else unless the law requires it.

Advertising and analytics

We may use Google Ads and Google Analytics to measure how many people find us through our ads and contact us. These tools can use cookies to count visits and enquiries. You can control cookies in your browser settings, and manage Google ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not go ahead are deleted within 12 months. For jobs we carry out, we keep records for as long as needed for guarantees, accounts and legal obligations.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you, or object to how we use them, by contacting us at the email above. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.
