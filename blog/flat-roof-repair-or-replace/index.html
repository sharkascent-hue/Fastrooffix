Flat roof: repair or replace?.

Pooling water, blisters or a leak in the extension? How to tell whether a repair will do or the roof needs replacing.

Signs a repair will do

A single split, a small blister or a leak around one detail such as an outlet or upstand can usually be repaired with torch-on felt patching or a liquid membrane.

Signs it needs replacing

Widespread cracking, many blisters, a soft or spongy deck underfoot, or repeated leaks in different places all point to a covering at the end of its life.

Why pooling water matters

Water that sits on a flat roof for days speeds up wear and finds any weak point. Sometimes the fix is improving the fall so the roof drains properly.

Your options for a new roof

Torch-on felt, EPDM rubber and fibreglass (GRP) are all good systems. The right one depends on the size of the roof, how it is used and your budget.
